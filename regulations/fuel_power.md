# Approved Document L Volume 1: Dwellings - Conservation of Fuel and Power

## Document Information
- **Title**: Approved Document L, Volume 1: Dwellings
- **Regulation**: Building Regulations 2010 (as amended)
- **Requirements**: Part L1 (Conservation of fuel and power), Part L2 (On-site generation of electricity)
- **Relevant Regulations**: 6, 22, 23, 24, 25, 25A, 25B, 26, 26A, 26C, 27, 27A, 27C, 28, 40, 40A, 43, 44, 44ZA
- **Effective Date**: 15 June 2022 (2021 edition incorporating 2023 amendments)
- **Scope**: New dwellings, extensions, and work to existing dwellings in England

## Summary
This approved document provides practical guidance on meeting energy efficiency requirements for dwellings. It establishes standards for limiting heat gains and losses through building fabric, specifies minimum efficiencies for fixed building services (heating, hot water, lighting), and requires energy performance calculations using the Standard Assessment Procedure (SAP 10).

Key objectives:
- Achieve nearly zero-energy building standards
- Limit CO₂ emissions and primary energy consumption
- Ensure high-quality construction (continuity of insulation, airtightness, minimal thermal bridging)
- Commission building services for optimal performance

## Compliance Routes

### 1. Energy Performance Calculations (Regulations 24-27C)
Compliance requires calculations at both design and completion stages using SAP 10.

**Three Target Metrics:**
1. **Target Primary Energy Rate (TPER)**: kWh primary energy/m²/year
2. **Target Emission Rate (TER)**: kgCO₂/m²/year
3. **Target Fabric Energy Efficiency Rate (TFEER)**: kWh/m²/year (fabric performance only)

**Notional Dwelling Specification** (baseline for targets):
- External walls: U = 0.18 W/(m²·K)
- Floors: U = 0.13 W/(m²·K)
- Roofs: U = 0.11 W/(m²·K)
- Windows/doors (>60% glazed): U = 1.2 W/(m²·K)
- Air permeability: 5 m³/(h·m²) at 50 Pa
- Heating: Gas boiler (89.5% SEDBUK 2009), radiators, 55°C flow temp
- Hot water: Separate time control, wastewater heat recovery on all showers
- Lighting: 80 lm/W efficacy
- PV: 40% of ground floor area (houses) or proportional (flats)

*Note: To meet TFEER, elements often need to exceed these minimums significantly.*

#### Existing Dwellings - New/Replacement Elements (Table 4.2)
- **Roof**: 0.15 W/(m²·K)
- **Wall**: 0.18 W/(m²·K)
- **Floor**: 0.18 W/(m²·K)
- **Windows**: 1.4 W/(m²·K) or Window Energy Rating Band B minimum
- **Rooflights**: 2.2 W/(m²·K)
- **Doors (>60% glazed)**: 1.4 W/(m²·K) or Doorset Energy Rating Band C minimum
- **Other doors**: 1.4 W/(m²·K) or Doorset Energy Rating Band B minimum

*Timber windows/doors: Transitional period to 14 June 2023, then full standards apply.*

#### Renovated Elements (Table 4.3)
Trigger: Renovating >50% of element surface OR major renovation (>25% of building envelope).

| Element | Threshold U-Value | Improved U-Value Required |
|---------|------------------|---------------------------|
| Roof | 0.35 | 0.16 |
| Wall (cavity fill) | 0.70 | 0.55 |
| Wall (internal/external) | 0.70 | 0.30 |
| Floor | 0.70 | 0.25 |

*Exception: If not technically/functionally feasible or payback >15 years, upgrade to lowest feasible U-value (generally not worse than 0.7).*

### 3. Building Services (Requirements L1(b) & L2)

#### Heating System Efficiencies

**Gas-fired (New Dwellings):**
- Minimum 92% efficiency (ErP/SEDBUK)
- Systems must be designed for maximum flow temperature of 55°C or lower (where feasible)

**Oil-fired (Existing Dwellings):**
- Regular boiler: 91% (ErP)
- Combi boiler: 86% (SEDBUK 2009)

**Heat Pumps:**
- Space heating COP: Minimum 3.0
- Water heating COP: Minimum 2.0
- Must be sized to meet full heating demand without electric backup
- Requires weather compensation or internal temperature control

**District Heat Networks:**
- Existing networks: Must not exceed 0.350 kgCO₂/kWh emission factor
- New networks: Must comply with AD L Volume 2 standards

#### Controls Requirements
- **Zoning**: Dwellings >150m² require minimum two independently controlled heating circuits
- **Thermostatic controls**: Each room requires thermostatic control (TRVs or room stats), except open-plan single-storey dwellings where living area >70%
- **Time control**: Separate time control for space heating and domestic hot water
- **Boiler interlock**: Required to switch off boiler and pump when no demand

#### Lighting
- **Internal**: Minimum 75 lumens per circuit-watt; local controls for each space
- **External**: Automatic daylight controls; occupancy controls required if efficacy ≤75 lm/W

#### On-site Electricity Generation (Requirement L2)
- Must be appropriately sized for site and infrastructure
- Requires effective controls and commissioning
- Replacement systems must maintain or exceed original capacity (unless demonstrated otherwise)

### 4. Airtightness (Regulation 43)
- **Minimum standard**: 8.0 m³/(h·m²) @ 50Pa (Table 4.1)
- **Testing**: Mandatory for every new dwelling
- **Methodology**: CIBSE TM23
- **Design target**: Often needs to be better than 8.0 to meet emission targets
- **Measured value**: Used in final SAP calculation

### 5. Consequential Improvements (Regulation 28)
Applies to existing dwellings >1,000m² undergoing:
- Extensions, or
- Initial provision of fixed building services, or
- Increase to installed capacity of fixed building services

Requirement: Improve overall energy efficiency where technically, functionally and economically feasible.

### 6. High-Efficiency Alternative Systems (Regulation 25A)
Before construction starts, analyse feasibility of:
- Decentralised renewable energy systems
- Cogeneration
- District/block heating or cooling
- Heat pumps

Document the analysis and provide to building control body.

## Duty Holders & Responsibilities

### Designer/Specifiers
- Calculate target rates using SAP 10 Appendix R
- Provide design stage BREL report (Building Regulations England Part L compliance report)
- Ensure continuity of insulation and minimal thermal bridging in details
- Specify airtightness strategy and air barrier locations

### Builders/Installers
- **Photographic evidence**: Take geo-located photos of:
  - Foundation/ground floor insulation continuity
  - External wall insulation and penetrations
  - Roof insulation at eaves and gables
  - Window/door positioning relative to insulation line
  - Airtightness details
  - Building services plant and pipework insulation
- **Construction**: Remove mortar snots, ensure tight-fit insulation, seal all penetrations
- **Pressure testing**: Arrange for testing by registered tester

### Energy Assessors
- Produce design stage and as-built BREL reports
- Sign off as-built calculations confirming accuracy and that photographic evidence has been reviewed
- Compare design vs. as-built specifications and document changes

### Building Owner/Developers
- Provide commissioning notices to building control body within 5 days of completion
- Provide operating and maintenance instructions (including Home User Guide) to occupiers
- Ensure as-built BREL report is signed by developer confirming construction matches specifications

## Key Rules

1. **Calculation Tool**: Must use SAP 10 (Standard Assessment Procedure) for all energy calculations.
2. **Three Pillars of Compliance**: Dwelling must achieve all three targets: Primary Energy Rate, Emission Rate, and Fabric Energy Efficiency Rate.
3. **Continuity**: Insulation must be continuous across all junctions; thermal bridging must be minimised (y-value ≤0.20 W/(m²·K) or calculated).
4. **Conservatories/Porches**: Exempt from Part L if ground level, ≤30m², thermally separated, and heating not extended into them.
5. **Listed Buildings**: Work need not comply if it would unacceptably alter character/appearance; otherwise comply to extent reasonably practicable.
6. **Pipework Insulation**: All hot water pipework must meet BS 5422 standards; primary circulation pipes require insulation.
7. **Hot Water Cylinders**: Must meet specific heat loss limits (e.g., 150 litres = 1.88 kWh/24h max).
8. **Commissioning**: All fixed building services and on-site generation must be commissioned by testing and adjusting to ensure optimal performance.
9. **Information**: Operating and maintenance instructions must be provided to owners within 5 days of completion, including Home User Guide for new dwellings.

## Exemptions & Special Cases

- **Historic/Traditional Dwellings**: Improvements should not cause long-term deterioration of vapour-permeable construction (wattle/daub, cob, lime render).
- **Change of Use**: Material change of use to dwelling requires compliance with Part L for the whole building or affected part.
- **Live/Work Units**: Treated as dwellings if commercial part can revert to domestic use (direct access, same thermal envelope, substantial proportion residential).
