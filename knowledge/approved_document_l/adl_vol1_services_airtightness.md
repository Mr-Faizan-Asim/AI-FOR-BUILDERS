# Approved Document L Vol 1: Building Services Heating & Controls – Chunk 3

Fabric standards require specific U-values for building elements, while building services must meet minimum efficiency standards to achieve compliance targets under Requirements L1(b) and L2. **Duty Holders:** Designers (specification), Installers (commissioning)

Heating system efficiencies vary by fuel type and dwelling status. For gas-fired systems in new dwellings, minimum efficiency is 92% (ErP/SEDBUK) and systems must be designed for maximum flow temperature of 55°C or lower where feasible. For oil-fired systems in existing dwellings, regular boilers require 91% (ErP) and combi boilers 86% (SEDBUK 2009).

Heat pumps must achieve a space heating COP minimum of 3.0 and water heating COP minimum of 2.0. They must be sized to meet full heating demand without electric backup and require weather compensation or internal temperature control. District heat networks must not exceed 0.350 kgCO₂/kWh emission factor for existing networks, while new networks must comply with AD L Volume 2 standards.

Controls requirements include: Zoning for dwellings greater than 150m² requiring minimum two independently controlled heating circuits. Each room requires thermostatic control (TRVs or room stats) except open-plan single-storey dwellings where living area exceeds 70%. Separate time control is required for space heating and domestic hot water. Boiler interlock is required to switch off boiler and pump when no demand exists.

# Approved Document L Vol 1: Lighting, Generation & Airtightness – Chunk 4

Controls requirements include thermostatic controls in each room and separate time controls for heating and hot water systems. **Duty Holders:** Designers, Builders, Installers

Lighting standards require minimum 75 lumens per circuit-watt for internal lighting with local controls for each space. External lighting requires automatic daylight controls and occupancy controls if efficacy is less than or equal to 75 lm/W. On-site electricity generation under Requirement L2 must be appropriately sized for site and infrastructure, requires effective controls and commissioning, and replacement systems must maintain or exceed original capacity unless demonstrated otherwise.

Airtightness under Regulation 43 requires a minimum standard of 8.0 m³/(h·m²) at 50Pa. Testing is mandatory for every new dwelling using CIBSE TM23 methodology. The design target often needs to be better than 8.0 to meet emission targets, and the measured value is used in final SAP calculation.

Consequential Improvements under Regulation 28 apply to existing dwellings greater than 1,000m² undergoing extensions, initial provision of fixed building services, or increases to installed capacity of fixed building services. The requirement is to improve overall energy efficiency where technically, functionally and economically feasible.

Before construction starts, Regulation 25A requires analysis of feasibility for decentralised renewable energy systems, cogeneration, district/block heating or cooling, and heat pumps. The analysis must be documented and provided to the building control body.
