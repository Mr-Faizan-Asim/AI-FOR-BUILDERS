# Approved Document L Vol 1: Duty Holders Design & Construction – Chunk 5

The building control body must receive documentation of high-efficiency alternative systems analysis, while duty holders must ensure compliance throughout design and construction phases. **Duty Holders:** Designers, Builders, Energy Assessors

Designers and specifiers must calculate target rates using SAP 10 Appendix R, provide design stage BREL report (Building Regulations England Part L compliance report), ensure continuity of insulation and minimal thermal bridging in details, and specify airtightness strategy and air barrier locations.

Builders and installers have critical on-site responsibilities. They must take geo-located photographic evidence of: foundation/ground floor insulation continuity, external wall insulation and penetrations, roof insulation at eaves and gables, window/door positioning relative to insulation line, airtightness details, and building services plant and pipework insulation. During construction, they must remove mortar snots, ensure tight-fit insulation, and seal all penetrations. They must also arrange for pressure testing by a registered tester to demonstrate airtightness compliance.

Energy assessors produce design stage and as-built BREL reports. They must sign off as-built calculations confirming accuracy and that photographic evidence has been reviewed, and compare design versus as-built specifications to document any changes.

# Approved Document L Vol 1: Owners, Key Rules & Exemptions – Chunk 6

Energy assessors verify as-built calculations and photographic evidence, while building owners and developers have final responsibilities for commissioning and documentation. **Duty Holders:** Owners, Developers, Assessors

Building owners and developers must provide commissioning notices to the building control body within 5 days of completion, provide operating and maintenance instructions (including Home User Guide) to occupiers, and ensure as-built BREL report is signed by developer confirming construction matches specifications.

Key Rules for compliance: 1) Calculation Tool: Must use SAP 10 for all energy calculations. 2) Three Pillars: Dwelling must achieve all three targets (Primary Energy Rate, Emission Rate, and Fabric Energy Efficiency Rate). 3) Continuity: Insulation must be continuous across all junctions; thermal bridging must be minimised (y-value ≤0.20 W/(m²·K) or calculated). 4) Conservatories/Porches: Exempt from Part L if ground level, ≤30m², thermally separated, and heating not extended into them. 5) Listed Buildings: Work need not comply if it would unacceptably alter character/appearance; otherwise comply to extent reasonably practicable. 6) Pipework Insulation: All hot water pipework must meet BS 5422 standards. 7) Hot Water Cylinders: Must meet specific heat loss limits (e.g., 150 litres = 1.88 kWh/24h max). 8) Commissioning: All fixed building services and on-site generation must be commissioned by testing and adjusting. 9) Information: Operating and maintenance instructions must be provided within 5 days of completion.

Exemptions and Special Cases: Historic/traditional dwellings should not suffer long-term deterioration of vapour-permeable construction (wattle/daub, cob, lime render). Change of use to a dwelling requires compliance for the whole building or affected part. Live/work units are treated as dwellings if the commercial part can revert to domestic use.

Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/conservation-of-fuel-and-power-approved-document-l
