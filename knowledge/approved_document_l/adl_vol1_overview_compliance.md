# Approved Document L Vol 1: Document Overview & Energy Calculations – Chunk 1

**Document:** Approved Document L, Volume 1: Dwellings – Conservation of Fuel and Power  
**Regulation:** Building Regulations 2010 (as amended)  
**Effective Date:** 15 June 2022 (2021 edition incorporating 2023 amendments)  
**Scope:** New dwellings, extensions, and work to existing dwellings in England  
**Duty Holders:** Designers/Specifiers, Energy Assessors

This approved document provides practical guidance on meeting energy efficiency requirements for dwellings. It establishes standards for limiting heat gains and losses through building fabric, specifies minimum efficiencies for fixed building services (heating, hot water, lighting), and requires energy performance calculations using the Standard Assessment Procedure (SAP 10).

Key objectives include achieving nearly zero-energy building standards, limiting CO₂ emissions and primary energy consumption, ensuring high-quality construction (continuity of insulation, airtightness, minimal thermal bridging), and commissioning building services for optimal performance.

Compliance requires calculations at both design and completion stages using SAP 10. Three Target Metrics must be met: 1) Target Primary Energy Rate (TPER) in kWh primary energy/m²/year, 2) Target Emission Rate (TER) in kgCO₂/m²/year, and 3) Target Fabric Energy Efficiency Rate (TFEER) in kWh/m²/year (fabric performance only).

The Notional Dwelling Specification serves as the baseline for targets and includes: External walls U = 0.18 W/(m²·K), Floors U = 0.13 W/(m²·K), Roofs U = 0.11 W/(m²·K), Windows/doors (>60% glazed) U = 1.2 W/(m²·K), Air permeability 5 m³/(h·m²) at 50 Pa, Heating via gas boiler (89.5% SEDBUK 2009) with radiators at 55°C flow temp, Hot water with separate time control and wastewater heat recovery on all showers, Lighting at 80 lm/W efficacy, and PV at 40% of ground floor area (houses) or proportional (flats).

# Approved Document L Vol 1: Fabric Standards – Chunk 2

The Notional Dwelling Specification establishes baseline U-values and system efficiencies for compliance calculations using SAP 10. **Duty Holders:** Designers (specification), Builders (installation)

Fabric Standards under Requirement L1(a) specify limiting standards for new dwellings. New dwellings must meet: Roof U-value maximum 0.16 W/(m²·K), Wall U-value maximum 0.26 W/(m²·K) including semi-exposed, Floor U-value maximum 0.18 W/(m²·K), Party wall U-value maximum 0.20 W/(m²·K), Swimming pool basin U-value maximum 0.25 W/(m²·K), Window area-weighted average U-value maximum 1.6 W/(m²·K), Rooflight U-value maximum 2.2 W/(m²·K) based on developed surface area, Doors (including glazed) U-value maximum 1.6 W/(m²·K), and Air Permeability maximum 8.0 m³/(h·m²) at 50Pa. Note: To meet TFEER, elements often need to exceed these minimums significantly.

For existing dwellings with new or replacement elements: Roof must achieve 0.15 W/(m²·K), Wall 0.18 W/(m²·K), Floor 0.18 W/(m²·K), Windows 1.4 W/(m²·K) or Window Energy Rating Band B minimum, Rooflights 2.2 W/(m²·K), Doors (>60% glazed) 1.4 W/(m²·K) or Doorset Energy Rating Band C minimum, and Other doors 1.4 W/(m²·K) or Doorset Energy Rating Band B minimum. Timber windows/doors had a transitional period to 14 June 2023, after which full standards apply.

Renovated elements trigger when renovating >50% of element surface OR major renovation (>25% of building envelope). Thresholds and improvements: Roof (existing 0.35) improve to 0.16, Wall cavity fill (existing 0.70) improve to 0.55, Wall internal/external (existing 0.70) improve to 0.30, Floor (existing 0.70) improve to 0.25. Exception: If not technically/functionally feasible or payback exceeds 15 years, upgrade to lowest feasible U-value (generally not worse than 0.7).
