# Approved Document B Volume 1: Fire Safety – Dwellings
## Requirement B1: Means of Escape – Dwellinghouses
**Source:** Approved Document B Volume 1, Section 2
**Building Type:** Dwellinghouses (single family homes)
**Key Standard:** REI 30 fire resistance rating for protected elements

## Escape Requirements by Storey Height

| Situation | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Ground storey** | Direct access to hall leading to final exit OR emergency escape window/door |
| **Upper storeys ≤4.5m above ground** | Emergency escape window (min 0.33m², 450mm x 450mm, sill ≤1100mm) OR direct access to protected stairway |
| **One storey &gt;4.5m above ground** | Protected stairway (REI 30) extending to final exit OR two ground level final exits separated by fire construction |
| **Two+ storeys &gt;4.5m above ground** | Alternative escape route from storey &gt;7.5m OR sprinkler system (BS 9251) |

## Key Construction Provisions

**Protected Stairways:**
- Must have cavity barriers OR fire resisting ceiling (EI 30) above
- Form primary escape route for multi-storey dwellings

**Lift Shafts:**
- Passenger lifts serving floors &gt;4.5m must be in protected stairway enclosure OR fire resisting shaft (REI 30)

**External Escape Stairs:**
- Fire resisting construction required within 1800mm horizontally and 9m vertically below

**Inner Rooms:**
- Permitted only for: kitchens, bathrooms, WCs, dressing rooms, or rooms with escape windows
- Risk assessment required for alternative uses

## Emergency Escape Windows
- Minimum clear opening area: 0.33m²
- Minimum dimensions: 450mm x 450mm  
- Maximum sill height: 1100mm above floor level
