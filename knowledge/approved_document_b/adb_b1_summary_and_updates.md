# Approved Document B Volume 1: Fire Safety – Dwellings
## Document Summary and Key Updates
**Source:** Approved Document B Volume 1 (2019 Edition incorporating 2020, 2022, 2025, 2026, and 2029 Amendments)
**Requirement:** B1 (Means of Warning and Escape) - General Overview
**Applicable To:** Dwellings, including blocks of flats
**Date Chunked:** 2026-01-31

This document provides practical guidance on how to meet the requirements of Part B (Fire Safety) of the Building Regulations 2010 for dwellings, including blocks of flats. It covers means of escape, fire spread (internal and external), and facilities for the fire service.

## Key Regulatory Updates Timeline

**2020 Amendments:**
- Sprinkler trigger height reduced from 30m to 11m
- Wayfinding signage introduced for blocks of flats over 11m

**2022 Amendments:**  
- Ban on combustible materials in external walls
- Secure information boxes and evacuation alert systems introduced

**2026 Amendments (Effective 30 Sept 2026):**
- Second stair requirement for buildings over 18m
- Evacuation lift provisions introduced

**2029 Amendments (Effective 2 Sept 2029):**
- Removal of national classes for fire resistance (European standards only)

## Scope of Requirements
The document addresses:
- Requirement B1: Means of Warning and Escape
- Requirement B2: Internal Fire Spread (Linings)  
- Requirement B3: Internal Fire Spread (Structure)
- Requirement B4: External Fire Spread
- Requirement B5: Access and Facilities for the Fire Service
- Regulation 38: Fire Safety Information
