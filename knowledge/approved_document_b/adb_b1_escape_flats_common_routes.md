# Approved Document B Volume 1: Fire Safety – Dwellings
## Requirement B1: Common Escape Routes and 2026 Amendments
**Source:** Approved Document B Volume 1, Section 3 (2026 Amendment effective 30 Sept 2026)
**Building Type:** Blocks of flats (common areas)
**Critical Update:** Second stair requirement for buildings ≥18m

## Common Escape Route Requirements by Building Height

| Building Height | Escape Route Requirements |
|-----------------|---------------------------|
| **≤11m with single stair** | Protected lobby/corridor required; max travel distance 7.5m (4.5m for small buildings) |
| **&gt;11m** | Protected lobby/corridor; smoke control required |
| **≥18m (2026 amendment)** | Minimum TWO common stairs required |

## Smoke Control Systems

**Natural Ventilation:**
- 1.5m² free area vents in corridors/lobbies at each storey level
- Automatic opening vents (AOVs) triggered by smoke detection

**Mechanical Systems:**
- Pressure differential systems to BS EN 12101-6
- Prevent smoke ingress to escape routes

## Wayfinding Signage (Blocks of flats &gt;11m) - 2020 Amendment

**Floor Identification Signs:**
- 50mm text height, 75mm numeral height
- Required at every landing level
- Visible in smoke conditions

**Flat Indicator Signs:**
- Show flat numbers and directional arrows
- Located 1.7m–2m above floor level
- Contrasting background colors required

## Evacuation Lifts (2026 Amendment)
- Located within evacuation shaft containing protected stairway
- Evacuation lift lobby provides refuge area with direct stair access
- Not directly accessible from flats or storage rooms
- For fire service use and assisted evacuation

## Protected Lobby/Corridor Standards
- REI 30 fire resistance minimum
- Direct access to protected stairway
- Smoke control integration required

## Transition to European Standards (2029)
- National classes for fire resistance removed 2 Sept 2029
- European classification standards (EN 13501) become sole reference
- REI ratings replace British standard references
