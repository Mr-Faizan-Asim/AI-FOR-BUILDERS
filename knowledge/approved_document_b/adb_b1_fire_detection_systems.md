# Approved Document B Volume 1: Fire Safety – Dwellings  
## Requirement B1: Means of Warning and Escape
### Section 1: Fire Detection and Alarm Systems
**Source:** Approved Document B Volume 1, Requirement B1
**Regulatory Reference:** Building Regulations 2010 Part B
**Intention:** Early warning systems for occupants; protection of escape routes from fire and smoke

Requirement B1 states: *The building shall be designed and constructed so that there are appropriate provisions for the early warning of fire, and appropriate means of escape in case of fire from the building to a place of safety outside the building capable of being safely and effectively used at all material times.*

## General Provisions for Fire Detection

**Minimum Standards (All Dwellings):**
- Grade D2 Category LD3 minimum (BS 5839-6)
- Smoke alarms: Mains operated to BS EN 14604 with battery backup
- Heat alarms: Mains operated to BS 5446-2 with battery backup

## Specific Building Type Requirements

**Large Dwellinghouses (over 200m² per storey):**
- 2 storeys: Grade A Category LD3
- 3+ storeys: Grade A Category LD2

**Extensions and New Habitable Rooms:**
- Fire detection required where there is no direct final exit
- Must maintain escape route safety standards

**Blocks of Flats:**
- Individual flat alarms required
- No communal system normally needed where effective compartmentation exists
- "Stay put" evacuation strategy provisions apply

**Specialized Housing:**
- Student accommodation: Separate detection per self-contained flat
- Sheltered housing: Systems connecting to central monitoring point required

## Compliance Standards Reference
- BS 5839-6: Fire detection and alarm systems for buildings
- BS EN 14604: Smoke alarm devices
- BS 5446-2: Heat alarm devices
