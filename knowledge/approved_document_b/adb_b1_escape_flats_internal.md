# Approved Document B Volume 1: Fire Safety – Dwellings
## Requirement B1: Means of Escape – Flats (Internal Planning)
**Source:** Approved Document B Volume 1, Section 3
**Building Type:** Blocks of flats (multi-unit residential)
**Strategy:** "Stay put" evacuation with simultaneous evacuation unlikely due to compartmentation

## Design Assumptions for Flats
- Fire likely to be contained within flat of origin through compartmentation
- No reliance on external rescue required
- Individual flat protection prioritized over building-wide evacuation

## Internal Planning Requirements by Flat Type

| Flat Type | Escape Requirement |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Single storey or upper storeys ≤4.5m** | Emergency escape window OR protected internal stairway |
| **Storeys &gt;4.5m – single storey flat** | Protected entrance hall (REI 30) OR restricted travel distance (9m max) OR alternative exit |
| **Multi-storey without ground entrance** | Alternative exit from each non-entrance storey OR protected stairway + sprinklers |

## Protected Entrance Halls (REI 30)
- Required for single storey flats above 4.5m without alternative exits
- Provides protected route to common areas
- Fire resistance rating: REI 30 (load-bearing, insulation, integrity)

## Travel Distance Restrictions
- Maximum 9m travel distance within flat when no alternative exit provided
- Applies to flats above 4.5m without protected entrance halls

## Alternative Exit Provisions
- Required for multi-storey flats without ground level entrance
- Must be available from each storey not having direct ground access
- OR protected internal stairway with sprinkler protection (BS 9251)

## Compartmentation Principles
- Effective compartmentation allows "stay put" strategy
- Reduces need for communal alarm systems
- Requires integrity of separating walls and floors
